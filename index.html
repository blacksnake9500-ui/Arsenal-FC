Arsenal Football Club

Arsenal Football Club, commonly referred to as Arsenal, is a professional football club based in Islington, London, England. The club was founded in 1886 and has a rich history of success in English football. Arsenal has won numerous domestic and international titles, including 13 league titles, 14 FA Cups, and 2 League Cups. The club is known for its attractive style of play and has a large fan base around the world.
